Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026  ·  Language: English (authoritative)  ·  ← Back to home

Padel Bracket is a free tournament app that works without an account. We do not sell data and never require registration. Tournament data is stored locally on your device. If you choose to log in (optional), we store your email address and a copy of your groups and tournaments so you can use them on another device.

1. Who we are

Padel Bracket ("we", "us", "our") is a free web application available at padel-bracket.com. It is an open-source project maintained by Danny de Vis.

2. Data we collect

We collect minimal data to understand how the app is used. Specifically:

  • Google Analytics 4 — only active if you explicitly accept cookies. Collects anonymised page views, session counts, and device type (no IP address stored). No personal profiles are created.
  • No account required — you can use every feature without giving us your name, email, phone number, or any personal identifier.
  • Optional account — only if you choose to log in. See section 4.
  • No tournament data on our servers by default — without an account, tournaments are stored only in your browser's localStorage, unless you share them live (section 3).

3. Live sharing (Supabase)

When you use the "Share" feature to generate a live-view link, the current tournament state is temporarily stored in a Supabase database to allow spectators to follow along. This data:

  • Contains only the tournament scores and player names you entered
  • Is identified by a random 6-character code, not by your identity
  • Is overwritten each time you push an update
  • Is not linked to any account, device identifier, or IP address on our end

Supabase processes this data as a sub-processor under its own privacy policy.

4. Optional account

You can log in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email address. There is no password. Logging in is optional; nothing in the app requires it. If you log in, we store in our Supabase database:

  • Your email address, and when you logged in (handled by Supabase Auth)
  • If you log in with Google: your Google account's name and email address as Google provides them to us. Google knows that you use Padel Bracket. If you don't want that, use the email code instead.
  • When you open the login panel, Google's sign-in button is loaded from Google, so Google sees your IP address at that moment. It is not loaded for visitors who never open the login panel.
  • A copy of what is on your device: your saved player groups, your own competitions, your running tournament, and the management keys of tournaments, signups and club competitions you created

Only you can read this copy: the database only returns it to the account that stored it. We use it for one thing: to put the same data on your other devices.

You can log out at any time, and delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account). Deleting removes your account and everything stored in it. What is on your device stays there, and links you shared keep working, because those are not tied to your account.

5. Cookies and localStorage

Padel Bracket uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to save:

  • Your current tournament state (so you can resume after closing the browser)
  • Your language preference
  • Your theme preference (dark/light)
  • Your saved player groups (if you use that feature)
  • Your analytics consent choice
  • If you logged in: your login session

Google Analytics is loaded only after you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. If you click "Decline", no analytics cookies are set and no tracking occurs.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Analytics 4 — analytics, consent-gated. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts (Inter) — loaded from Google's CDN. Google may log the request (your IP, browser). Google Fonts privacy
  • Supabase — used when you share a tournament, and for the optional account. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — loaded when you open the login panel, and used only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google Privacy Policy

7. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU or EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access, rectify, or erase any data we hold about you
  • Withdraw consent for analytics at any time (clear your browser's localStorage key padel-consent to reset)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Without an account we hold no personal data about you, so most GDPR rights can be exercised by simply clearing your browser data. With an account, you can delete it yourself in the app at any time.

8. Data retention

Local browser data (localStorage) persists until you clear your browser storage. Account data is kept until you delete your account. Supabase live-share data is overwritten on each update and is not permanently retained. Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months (Google's minimum setting).

9. Children

Padel Bracket is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
