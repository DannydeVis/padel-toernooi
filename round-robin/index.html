Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load, PadelBracket works fully offline. The tournament is saved locally on your device, even if you close the browser or lose internet on the court.

No. No registration or login required. Open the app and start your Round Robin directly — completely free.

How many players does Round Robin work for?

PadelBracket's Round Robin works for 4 to 16 players (2 to 8 teams). Every team plays every other team at least once.

How are Round Robin standings calculated?

Standings are based on number of wins first, then total games won. In case of a tie, the head-to-head result between tied teams determines the ranking.

What is the difference between Round Robin and Americano?

In Round Robin, pairs are fixed and every team plays every other team. In Americano, partners rotate every round and points are counted per individual player. Round Robin suits competitive formats; Americano is ideal for casual social sessions.

How do I organize a padel Round Robin tournament?

To organize a padel Round Robin tournament, open PadelBracket, enter the team names, and the app generates the full schedule so every team plays every other team. Scores are entered after each match and the standings update automatically — no spreadsheets or manual planning needed.

How many teams can play in a Round Robin padel tournament?

PadelBracket's Round Robin format supports 4 to 16 players, or 2 to 8 teams of two. Every team plays every other team at least once, and the app automatically builds a fair schedule that fits the number of courts available.
