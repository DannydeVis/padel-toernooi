Frequently asked questions

Is PadelBracket free?

Yes, PadelBracket is completely free. No subscription, no account, no paywall. All features are available immediately.

Which tournament formats are supported?

PadelBracket supports four formats: knockout bracket (groups → semi-finals → final), Americano (rotating partners, points per player), Mexicano (partners based on standings) and Round Robin (everyone plays everyone).

What is the difference between Americano and Mexicano?

In Americano, partners are determined in advance via a fixed rotation — everyone plays exactly once together. In Mexicano, partners are reassigned after each round based on current standings: rank 1 and 2 play together against rank 3 and 4. Mexicano gets more competitive as the tournament progresses.

What does presentation mode do?

Presentation mode opens a fullscreen scoreboard, ideal for a TV or projector in the clubhouse. You see the live standings and all active matches with live scores. The screen refreshes automatically every 3 seconds.

How can participants watch live?

Use the Share button in the app to generate a unique link. Participants open it on their phone and see the live standings — no account, no installation. Scores update as you enter them.

Can I export the results?

Yes. After the tournament you can export the final standings and all match results as CSV (for Excel or Google Sheets) or print as PDF. The export button appears as soon as the first match is played.

How many players can I use?

For Americano and Mexicano up to 24 players. For a team bracket 4 to 9 teams (8–18 players). With an odd number of players (5, 7, 9…) one player rotates on a bye per round.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. After the first load, PadelBracket works fully offline. The tournament is saved locally on your device, even if you close the browser.

Which languages is the app available in?

PadelBracket is available in Dutch, English, French, German, Spanish and Swedish. The app automatically detects your device language.
