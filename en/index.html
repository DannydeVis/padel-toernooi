Frequently asked questions

Is PadelBracket free?

Yes, PadelBracket is completely free. No subscription, no account, no paywall. All features are available immediately.

Which tournament formats are supported?

PadelBracket supports four formats: knockout bracket (groups → semi-finals → final), Americano (rotating partners, points per player), Mexicano (partners re-paired by standings each round) and Round Robin (everyone plays everyone).

What is the difference between Americano and Mexicano?

In Americano, partner rotations are fixed in advance using a circle algorithm — every player partners every other player exactly once. In Mexicano, partners are re-paired dynamically after every round: rank 1 plays with rank 2 against rank 3 and 4, rank 5 with rank 6 against rank 7 and 8, and so on. This means stronger players increasingly face each other, making Mexicano more competitive as the tournament progresses.

How many players can I use?

For a team tournament, a minimum of 4 players (2 teams). For Americano, the app works with multiples of 4: 8, 12, 16 or more players.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. After the first load, PadelBracket works fully offline. The tournament is saved locally on your device, even if you close the browser.

Do I need to create an account?

No. No registration or login required. Open the app and start your tournament directly.

Which languages is the app available in?

PadelBracket is available in Dutch, English, French, German, Spanish and Swedish. The app automatically detects your device language.
