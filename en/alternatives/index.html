Why this page exists

Picking the right padel tournament tool

If you search for a "padel tournament app" or "padel Americano generator", you'll find several options — generic esports/sports bracket platforms like Challonge and Score7, and dedicated padel tools like americanopadel.app and bracketmaker.app, alongside PadelBracket. They all solve a version of the same problem: turning a list of names into a schedule, and a schedule into a result.

The table below compares them on seven concrete questions an organiser actually asks before a tournament: Is it free? Do I need an account? Does it work without wifi on the court? Does it support Americano and Mexicano specifically? Can I show live scores on a TV? Can I export a PDF afterwards? The information is based on what each provider publishes about their own product. Where something couldn't be confirmed from public sources, it's marked "Unknown" rather than guessed — and if anything below is out of date, let us know and it will be corrected.
