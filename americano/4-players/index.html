Americano for 4 players

With 4 players, PadelBracket generates exactly 3 rounds on 1 court. Every player partners with every other player exactly once — the purest form of Americano.

There are no byes: all 4 players are on the court every round. A typical 4-player session takes 30–45 minutes depending on how many points you play per match.

Enter the 4 names in the app, tap Start, and the full schedule is generated instantly.

FAQ

How does Americano work with 4 players?

All 4 players play on 1 court. In 3 rounds, each player has every other player as a partner exactly once. No byes. The player with the most points across all rounds wins.

Do I need an account?

No. PadelBracket is completely free — no account, no registration, no paywall. Open the app and start immediately.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load, PadelBracket works fully offline. The tournament is saved locally on your device, even without internet on the court.
