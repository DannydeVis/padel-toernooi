FAQ

How are byes handled with 10 players?

With 10 players, 2 players rotate on a bye each round automatically — PadelBracket handles this for you. The bye rotation is distributed as evenly as possible so every player sits out roughly the same number of times.

Do I need an account?

No. PadelBracket is completely free — no account, no registration, no paywall. Open the app and start immediately.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load, PadelBracket works fully offline. The tournament is saved locally on your device, even without internet on the court.
