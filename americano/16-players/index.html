Americano for 16 players

With 16 players, PadelBracket fills 4 courts every round. No one sits out — all 16 are playing simultaneously. You can play any number of rounds; the leaderboard is always up to date.

A popular 16-player format plays 5–7 rounds, which fits comfortably in a 2-hour slot. With 15 rounds you complete the full partner rotation, but most events stop well before that.

FAQ

How does Americano work with 16 players?

16 players fill 4 courts simultaneously every round — no byes. Partners rotate following the Americano schedule. Play as many rounds as you like; the leaderboard updates after each one.

Do I need an account?

No. PadelBracket is completely free — no account, no registration, no paywall. Open the app and start immediately.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load, PadelBracket works fully offline. The tournament is saved locally on your device, even without internet on the court.
