Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load, PadelBracket works fully offline. The tournament is saved locally on your device, even if you close the browser or lose internet on the court.

Do I need an account?

No. No registration or login required. Open the app and start your Americano directly — completely free.

How many players does padel Americano work for?

PadelBracket's Americano works for 4 or more players — even or odd. With an even count divisible by 4 (4, 8, 12…) everyone plays every round. With other counts, 1 or 2 players rotate on a bye each round automatically.

How does Americano scoring work?

Points are counted per individual player after each round. Partners rotate so everyone plays with and against everyone. The player with the highest total points at the end wins the Americano.

What is the difference between Americano and Round Robin?

In Americano, partners rotate every round — you always have a new partner. In Round Robin, pairs are fixed and every team plays every other team. Americano is ideal for social sessions; Round Robin suits competitive club formats.

What is the difference between Americano and Mexicano?

In Americano, the partner rotation is pre-determined before the tournament starts. In Mexicano, partners are re-paired after each round based on the current standings — the top two players on the leaderboard play against players three and four, and so on. Mexicano gets increasingly competitive as the tournament progresses.

How long does a padel Americano tournament take?

A padel Americano with 8 players on 2 courts usually takes 90 minutes to 2 hours, depending on match length. In PadelBracket you can set a points target or a timer per round, so the schedule automatically fits the court time you have available.

What is the best padel format for an afternoon with friends?

Americano is widely considered the best padel format for a casual group, because partners rotate every round so nobody is stuck playing with or against the same people all day.
