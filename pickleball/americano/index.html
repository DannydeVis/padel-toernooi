Frequently asked questions

How does pickleball Americano work?

In pickleball Americano, partners rotate every round on a pre-determined schedule. Points are tracked per individual player. After each game, partners change so everyone plays with and against everyone. The player with the most total points wins.

What is the difference between pickleball Americano and round robin?

In Americano, partners rotate every round and points are tracked per individual. In round robin, pairs are fixed and every pair plays every other pair. Americano is ideal for social mixers. Round robin suits sessions with fixed pairs.

What scoring does pickleball Americano use?

PadelBracket defaults to a target score of 11 with win by 2 enabled. You can change the target to 15 or 21 and toggle win by 2 on or off in the setup wizard. Rally scoring is used throughout.

Can I export pickleball Americano results to DUPR?

Yes. After completing a pickleball Americano session, you can export results as a CSV file with match date, player names and scores, ready to use with DUPR's bulk match import. You select the score type and link players to their DUPR ID in the DUPR import screen.

How many players do I need for pickleball Americano?

PadelBracket's pickleball Americano works for 4 or more players. With multiples of 4, everyone plays every round. With other counts, 1 or 2 players rotate on a bye each round automatically.
