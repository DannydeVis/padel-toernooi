Frequently asked questions

What formats does the pickleball generator support?

PadelBracket supports Round Robin, Americano and Mexicano for pickleball. All three formats are free with no account required.

What scoring does the pickleball generator use?

The default target score is 11 with win by 2 enabled. You can change the target to 15 or 21 in the setup wizard. Win by 2 can be toggled on or off. Rally scoring is used throughout.

Can I export pickleball results to DUPR?

Yes. After completing a pickleball Americano, Mexicano, Round Robin or King of the Court session, you can export results as a CSV file with match date, player names and scores, ready to use with DUPR's bulk match import. You select the score type and link players to their DUPR ID in the DUPR import screen.

Does the pickleball tournament app work offline?

Yes. PadelBracket is a Progressive Web App. After the first load, it works fully offline on the court. The tournament state is saved locally even if you close the browser or lose internet.

Do I need an account to use the pickleball generator?

No. PadelBracket is completely free with no signup, no login and no payment required. Open the app, select Pickleball, and your tournament is running in under a minute.

How many players does the pickleball generator support?

The pickleball generator supports 4 or more players for Americano and Mexicano. Round Robin supports 4 to 24 players. With odd counts, the app automatically assigns byes.

What is pickleball open play and how does this app help?

Pickleball open play is a social mixer format where players rotate partners each round. PadelBracket's Americano format automates the rotation schedule, tracks individual points, and shows a live leaderboard, so the organizer can run the whole session from their phone.
