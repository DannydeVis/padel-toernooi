Frequently asked questions

What is a padel club competition in PadelBracket?

A club competition links several tournament sessions to one fixed group of players, so results carry over from evening to evening instead of starting fresh each time.

How does the points standings work across multiple evenings?

Each session's results add placement points to a running standings table. The table shows who has scored the most points across every evening played so far.

What is the ELO rating based on?

The ELO rating is calculated from each player's final placement in every session, recalculated from the full history each time a new session is added, so it reflects performance over time rather than a single evening.

How does the ladder with challenges work?

Players are listed on a ladder in ranked positions. A player can challenge someone ranked above them within a set range, and if the challenger wins, they swap positions on the ladder.

Who can challenge whom on the ladder?

The organizer sets an allowed challenge range, for example up to 3 positions above the challenger. Anyone further up the ladder cannot be challenged directly.

Can I share the standings with the group?

Yes. PadelBracket can export the standings as an image with the podium, the top 10 and a QR link to the live table, ready to share in a chat group.

Do players need an account to see the standings?

No. The standings can be viewed through a shared link or the exported image, without any account or app install.

Is the club competition feature free?

Yes. Setting up a club competition, the points standings, the ELO rating, the ladder and the shareable standings card are all completely free.
