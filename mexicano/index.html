Frequently asked questions

What is Mexicano padel?

Mexicano is a padel tournament format where partners are reassigned after each round based on the current standings. Rank 1 and 2 play together against rank 3 and 4, and so on. This makes the tournament progressively more competitive as the rounds go on.

What is the difference between Americano and Mexicano?

In Americano, the partner rotation is pre-determined before the tournament starts. In Mexicano, partners are re-paired after each round based on the current standings — the top two players always play together. Mexicano gets increasingly competitive as the tournament progresses.

How many players can play Mexicano?

PadelBracket supports Mexicano for 4 to 24 players. With multiples of 4 (4, 8, 12…) everyone plays every round. With other counts, one or two players rotate on a bye each round automatically.

Do I need an account?

No. PadelBracket is completely free — no account, no subscription, no paywall. Open the app and start your Mexicano directly.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load, PadelBracket works fully offline. The tournament is saved locally on your device, even if you close the browser or lose internet on the court.
