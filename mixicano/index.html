Frequently asked questions

What is Mixicano padel?

Mixicano (Mixed Americano) is a padel tournament format where every team is always composed of one man and one woman. Partners rotate every round automatically, just like in regular Americano, but the app ensures the gender balance is always maintained. It is the ideal format for mixed-gender groups.

What is the difference between Mixicano and Americano?

In regular Americano, partners rotate randomly or by a fixed schedule regardless of gender. In Mixicano, the rotation is constrained so that every team always has exactly one man and one woman. This makes Mixicano ideal for mixed groups where gender balance matters.

How many players do you need for Mixicano?

You need at least 2 men and 2 women (4 players total). PadelBracket supports Mixicano for any combination as long as there are at least 2 of each gender. With unequal numbers of men and women, some players will rotate on a bye each round.

Do I need an account to use the Mixicano generator?

No. PadelBracket is completely free, no account, no subscription, no paywall. Open the app and start your Mixicano tournament directly.

Does the Mixicano generator work without internet?

Yes. After the first load, the app works fully offline. Your tournament is saved locally on your device, even if you close the browser or lose internet on the court.
